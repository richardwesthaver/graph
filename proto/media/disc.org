A trademark used by [[id:a6a352c3-a4cc-4553-8107-bfefde8cdcd6][Sony]] and [[id:7c836fa5-de1a-4075-9ff3-1fde1a5910bb][Philips]] for their system used to
digitally encode audio for the SACD.

In 2005 Philips sold its DSD tool division to [[wikipedia:Sonic_Studio][Sonic Studio]].

DSD uses delta-sigma modulation (a form of pulse-density modulation
encoding). Signals are encoded in single-bit sequences at a sample
rate of ~2.8224 MHz~. 64x the SR of standard CD audio @ 44.1kHz but
with 1-bit samples instead of 16.

#+begin_quote
DSD is simply a format for storing a delta-sigma signal without
applying a decimation process that converts the signal to a PCM
signal.
#+end_quote
