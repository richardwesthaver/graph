Thread is a networking protocol that provies a reliable and efficient
way for Matter devices to communicate with each other.

(Matter is the 'language', Thread is the 'network')

- IPv6-based
- low-power mesh networking
- used as the transport for Matter
  - AKA Matter over Thread
