Fast and flexible compression format, our personal preference (most of
the time).
- developed at Facebook
- designed to give compression ratio comparable to DEFLATE but faster
  (especially for decompression).
- open-sourced C reference impl [2016-08-31 Wed]
- at max compression levels gets close to lzma,lzham, and ppmx. performs better than lza or bzip2.
