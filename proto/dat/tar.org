Tarfiles are often compressed, resulting in a ~tarball~. Unlike [[id:44562f82-60b6-4d89-9346-c0d5525e86c8][zip]]
files, compression and archival are two separate processes. This can
have some pretty significant performance implications so users beware!
(especially on Win*..)
