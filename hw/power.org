- Ah (Amp-hours) ≠ Amps: A 6.0Ah battery stores more energy but
  doesn’t necessarily output more current—its discharge rate (e.g.,
  30A) determines peak power.
- Compatibility isn’t universal: Older NiCad batteries (e.g., RB18L)
  output fewer amps than modern lithium-ion models, struggling with
  newer high-demand tools.
