#+begin_quote
While working at Boeing Research Labs, Rudolf Bayer and Edward
M. McCreight invented B-trees to efficiently manage index pages for
large random-access files. The basic assumption was that indices would
be so voluminous that only small chunks of the tree could fit in main
memory. Bayer and McCreight's paper Organization and maintenance of
large ordered indices was first circulated in July 1970 and later
published in Acta Informatica.
#+end_quote

#+begin_quote
Searching on one number is a solved problem; searching on two or more,
and asking for locations that are nearby in both x and y directions,
requires craftier algorithms.
#+end_quote

- used for spatial access methods
  - indexing multi-dimensional info such as geocoordinates, polygons, etc.
- key insight: group nearby objects and represent them with their
  =minimum bounding rectangle= in the next higher level of the tree
- like B-Tree, is a balanced search tree
- [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_bounding_rectangle][Minimum bounding rectangle (MBR)]]
*** Hilbert R-Tree
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       9da67ff9-b19f-47a9-adee-59b660672adc
:CREATED:  <2025-05-07 Wed 17:13>
:END:
:EDGES:
[2025-05-07 Wed 17:13] -- [[wikipedia:Hilbert R-tree][wiki]]
[2025-05-07 Wed 17:15] -> [[id:4240d8d8-a646-4372-8bb5-37d029b325c2][B+ Tree]]
:END:
Can be thought of as an extension to B+ Trees for multidemnsional objects.
- performance depends on the quality of the algorithm that clusters
  the data rectangles on a node.
  - Hilbert curve used for linear ordering on the data rectangles
- two types: static and dynamic
  - both use hilbert curve for better ordering
    - optimize for minimizing the area  of the resulting MBRs
- Packed Hilbert R-trees suitable for static DBs - very rare or no
  updates at all
- Dynamic Hilbert R-tree suitable for dynamic database with real-time
  query/update/insertion/deletion
  - every node has well-defined set of sibling nodes via proposed
    ordering on the R-tree nodes
  - sorts rectangles according to the hilbert value (lenght of the
    hilbert curve from the origin to a point) of the center (MBR)
  - given the ordering, and thus the set of siblings, deferred splitting can be used
  - by adjusting the split policy, can achieve a degree of space
    utilization as high as desired - unlike other R-tree variants.
