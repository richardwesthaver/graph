*** Hausdorff Dimension
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       4171e0af-f048-4973-9d33-96b1f2b17548
:CREATED:  <2025-05-07 Wed 16:53>
:AKA: Topological Dimension
:END:
:EDGES:
[2025-05-07 Wed 16:53] -- [[wikipedia:Hausdorff_dimension][wiki]]
:END:
A /measure of roughness/, or more specifically *fractal dimension*
introduced in 1918 by Felix Hausdorff.

- single point = 0
- line segment = 1
- square = 2
- cube = 3
