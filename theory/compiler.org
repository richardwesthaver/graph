#+begin_quote
The polyhedral method treats each loop iteration within nested loops
as lattice points inside mathematical objects called polyhedra,
performs affine transformations or more general non-affine
transformations such as tiling on the polytopes, and then converts the
transformed polytopes into equivalent, but optimized (depending on
targeted optimization goal), loop nests through polyhedra scanning.
#+end_quote
